# Sub-epic 4.3: Attachments

**Parent:** [Epic 4: Expenses](../4.0-epic.md)

**Goal:** Members attach files such as receipts and invoices to expenses, and only members of the owning household can read them.

**Tasks:**

- [4.3.1 Implement household file storage](4.3.1-file-storage.md)
- [4.3.2 Attach files to expenses](4.3.2-expense-attachments.md)

**Sub-epic done when:**

- [ ] Over HTTP, a member uploads a receipt photo, attaches it to an expense, downloads it through a short-lived link, and removes it.
- [ ] A member of another household can't list, download, or attach that file, even with its ID.
