# 4.1.3 Add expense persistence and create/read endpoints

**Epic:** [4.1 Ledger](4.1.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [4.1.1](4.1.1-categories.md), [4.1.2](4.1.2-allocation-engine.md)

**Goal:** An expense is stored and returned as one consistent aggregate: total, payers, allocation input, and calculated splits.

**Scope:** Expense, payer, allocation-input, and split tables with their migrations; a transactional repository; and the create, get, and list endpoints.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The schema has foreign keys, checks requiring `expenses.total_amount_cents > 0`, `expense_payers.amount_cents > 0`, and `expense_splits.amount_cents >= 0`, strategy checks, and indexes for household listing and per-member/currency accounting. Exact allocation amounts are non-negative, and adjustments may be signed. Children inherit the expense's currency. Participant order is stored explicitly.
- [ ] The referenced-category deletion policy is chosen and documented here: reject deletion or clear expense references. This task adds the corresponding foreign-key constraint, updates the 4.1.1 DELETE endpoint, and tests the behavior with persisted expenses. Rejected deletion maps to HTTP 409 with `ApiError`, including a foreign-key conflict caused by a concurrent reference, rather than a raw database error.
- [ ] Create derives the author and timestamps from the server. It validates payers, participants, and category against the household and the 1.1 lifecycle rules, and calculates splits with the allocation engine. Client-supplied splits are rejected.
- [ ] Multiple payers are supported independently of the allocation participants, and duplicate payers or participants are rejected.
- [ ] Get and list return complete contract-shaped aggregates, and the list is paginated deterministically.
- [ ] Tests cover aggregate round trips, valid zero split amounts, exact numeric/date conversion, cross-household references, and rollback when a child-row write fails.
