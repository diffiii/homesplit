# 4.2.1 Record settlement payments and expose history

**Epic:** [4.2 Settlements](4.2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [2.2](../../2-households/2.2-feature-settings.md), [4.1.2](../4.1-ledger/4.1.2-allocation-engine.md)

**Goal:** Members record payments made outside HomeSplit as independent ledger entries, not as flags on expense splits.

**Scope:** A settlement table with its migration, a record-payment endpoint, and paginated payment history. This task implements the 1.1 recording permissions and decides whether overpayment is allowed and how duplicates and corrections work. Any chosen balance-based restriction is implemented and tested in 4.2.2, so this task does not depend on balance computation. If that policy needs new fields or endpoints, propose a contract change first.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A payment stores the household, distinct payer and payee, a positive amount in minor units, currency, a nullable note, the creator from the session, and a server timestamp. Constraints and history indexes are in place.
- [ ] Parties must be eligible under 1.1 based on current membership, preserved membership history, or participation in this household's ledger. Eligibility uses the 2.1 helpers without balance aggregation, so historical members can settle debts before 4.2.2 exists. Amounts follow the 4.1.2 currency limits, and the expenses module must be enabled.
- [ ] Duplicate-submission and correction behavior is documented, and identical legitimate payments are not silently merged.
- [ ] Tests cover authorization, historical-member eligibility after removal, invalid and cross-household parties, stable pagination, rollback, and the chosen retry policy.
