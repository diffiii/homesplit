# 4.2.1 Record settlement payments and expose history

**Epic:** [4.2 Settlements](4.2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [2.2](../../2-households/2.2-feature-settings.md), [4.1.2](../4.1-ledger/4.1.2-allocation-engine.md)

**Goal:** Members record payments made outside HomeSplit as independent ledger entries, not as flags on expense splits.

**Scope:** A settlement table with its migration, a record-payment endpoint, and paginated payment history. This task also decides who may record payments, whether overpayment is allowed, and how duplicates and corrections work. If that policy needs new fields or endpoints, propose a contract change first.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A payment stores the household, distinct payer and payee, a positive amount in minor units, currency, a nullable note, the creator from the session, and a server timestamp. Constraints and history indexes are in place.
- [ ] Parties must be eligible under 1.1 (including historical members with outstanding balances). Amounts follow the 4.1.2 currency limits, and the expenses module must be enabled.
- [ ] Duplicate-submission and correction behavior is documented, and identical legitimate payments are not silently merged.
- [ ] Tests cover authorization, invalid parties, stable pagination, rollback, and the chosen retry policy.
