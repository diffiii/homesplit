# 7.3 Scan receipts into editable drafts

**Epic:** [7 AI and receipts](7.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [7.1](7.1-ai-provider.md), [4.3.1](../4-expenses/4.3-attachments/4.3.1-file-storage.md)

**Goal:** A member uploads a receipt photo and gets an editable draft with the merchant, date, currency, total, and line items.

**Scope:** Receipt and receipt-item tables with their migrations, an endpoint that starts extraction from an uploaded attachment, background processing, and endpoints to get, list, edit, and discard drafts. A vision-capable model extracts structured data directly, without a separate OCR step, unless 7.1 records otherwise.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A receipt has a status (`processing`, `ready`, `failed`, `confirmed`). Extraction runs in the background, the client polls for the status, and a failed extraction can be retried or filled in manually.
- [ ] Each item stores its position, name, quantity (decimal, to allow weighed products), unit price, line discount, and line total in minor units. Receipt-level discounts are stored separately. Discount lines such as "Rabat" are attached to the item they reduce.
- [ ] The draft reports the difference between the sum of items and the receipt total, so the client can show it.
- [ ] Members can edit the header and add, change, remove, and reorder items until the receipt is confirmed. A confirmed receipt is read-only.
- [ ] Tests with the fake adapter cover a successful extraction, invalid model output, a mismatched total, editing, and access from another household.
