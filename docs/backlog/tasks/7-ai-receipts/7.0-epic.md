# Epic 7: AI and receipts

**Backlog:** [Backend backlog](../../backend-backlog.md) · **Depends on:** [Epic 4](../4-expenses/4.0-epic.md), [Epic 6](../6-shopping/6.0-epic.md)

**Goal:** Members photograph a receipt, assign its line items to people, and get a categorized expense with exact splits, plus suggestions for which shopping-list items were bought.

This epic is planned for after the core epics. AI only proposes: it suggests categories, extracts receipt drafts, and matches shopping items. A member reviews every proposal before anything is saved. Confirmed receipts become ordinary expenses through the [4.1.3](../4-expenses/4.1-ledger/4.1.3-expense-persistence-create-read.md) create path, and the [4.1.2](../4-expenses/4.1-ledger/4.1.2-allocation-engine.md) allocation engine calculates the amounts, so balances and settlements need no changes.

- [7.2 Suggest expense categories](7.2-category-suggestions.md)

- [7.4 Assign receipt items and create the expense](7.4-receipt-expense.md)
- [7.5 Match receipts to the shopping list](7.5-shopping-match.md)

**Epic done when:**

- [ ] Over HTTP with the fake AI provider, a member uploads a receipt, corrects one item, assigns items to three members (one item to one person, one split equally, one split by units), confirms it, and the resulting expense has the correct category, exact splits, and the receipt as an attachment.
- [ ] Confirming suggested shopping matches marks exactly those items as purchased.
- [ ] With the AI module disabled, no data is sent to the provider, and expenses can still be created manually.
