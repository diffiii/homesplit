# 6.2 Implement purchase, unpurchase, and clear-purchased

**Epic:** [6 Shopping](6.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [6.1](6.1-shopping-crud.md)

**Goal:** Purchases record who bought an item and when, and clearing the list removes only that household's purchased items.

**Scope:** Purchase and unpurchase action endpoints, plus a clear-purchased endpoint.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] New items are unpurchased, with null purchase metadata. Purchase sets the buyer and time atomically, and unpurchase clears both.
- [ ] A database check enforces the contracted purchase-state invariant.
- [ ] Clear deletes only purchased items in the requested household and returns the actual `deletedCount`.
- [ ] Tests cover repeated actions, clear running concurrently with purchase or unpurchase, authorization, and rollback.
