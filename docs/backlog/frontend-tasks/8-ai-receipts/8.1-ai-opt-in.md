# 8.1 Let admins opt in to AI

**Epic:** [8 AI and receipts](8.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [3.6](../3-households/3.6-module-settings.md), [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53)

**Goal:** A household decides knowingly whether its data may be sent to an AI provider.

**Scope:** The `ai` module flag in household settings and the gating of every AI feature.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The `ai` toggle is off by default, can be changed by admins and owners, and explains which data is sent (receipt images, expense text) and to whom, following the [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53) privacy policy.
- [ ] AI features are hidden when the flag is off or when the backend reports AI as unavailable. How the client learns the second case is agreed with [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53).
- [ ] Usage-limit errors from [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53) are explained, and the manual path stays available.

**References:** [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53), [feature catalog](../../../../packages/contracts/src/features/catalog.ts)
