# Epic 8: AI and receipts

**Backlog:** [Frontend backlog](../../frontend-backlog.md) · **Depends on:** [5 Expenses](../5-expenses/5.0-epic.md), [7 Shopping](../7-shopping/7.0-epic.md) · **Backend:** [#52](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/52)

**Goal:** Members photograph a receipt, fix what the AI misread, assign items to people, and get an expense with exact splits, plus suggestions for which shopping-list items were bought.

This epic is planned for after the core epics, like [#52](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/52). AI only proposes; the UI always lets members review and change a proposal before anything is saved, and every flow still works by hand with AI turned off.

**Tasks:**

- [8.1 Let admins opt in to AI](8.1-ai-opt-in.md)
- [8.2 Suggest categories in the expense form](8.2-category-suggestions.md)
- [8.3 Capture receipts and edit drafts](8.3-receipt-capture-drafts.md)
- [8.4 Assign receipt items and confirm the expense](8.4-receipt-assignment.md)
- [8.5 Review shopping matches](8.5-shopping-match.md)

**Epic done when:**

- [ ] Against the fake AI provider, a Playwright test reproduces the [#52](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/52) scenario through the UI: upload a receipt, correct an item, assign items to three members, confirm, and check the expense and its attachment.
- [ ] With AI turned off, no AI features are shown and receipts can't be scanned, while expenses and shopping work as before.
