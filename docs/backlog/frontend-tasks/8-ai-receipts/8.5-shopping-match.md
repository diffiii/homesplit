# 8.5 Review shopping matches

**Epic:** [8 AI and receipts](8.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [8.4](8.4-receipt-assignment.md), [7.2](../7-shopping/7.2-purchase-clear.md), [#57](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/57)

**Goal:** After a receipt is confirmed, members tick off the shopping-list items they bought in one step.

**Scope:** A review screen for the [#57](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/57) suggestions and its bulk purchase endpoint.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Suggested matches are listed next to the receipt item they match, preselected, and can be unselected.
- [ ] Other unpurchased items can be added by hand, which is the whole screen when AI is off.
- [ ] After the bulk purchase, items skipped because they were bought or deleted in the meantime are reported.

**References:** [#57](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/57)
