# 8.3 Capture receipts and edit drafts

**Epic:** [8 AI and receipts](8.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [8.1](8.1-ai-opt-in.md), [5.3.1](../5-expenses/5.3-attachments/5.3.1-file-upload.md), [#55](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/55)

**Goal:** A member photographs a receipt and gets an editable draft without waiting on a blank screen.

**Scope:** Starting a scan from an upload, following its status, and editing the draft.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A "scan receipt" action uploads a photo with 5.3.1 and starts extraction. While the receipt is `processing`, the screen polls with backoff and shows progress.
- [ ] A `failed` receipt can be retried or filled in by hand.
- [ ] A `ready` draft shows merchant, date, currency, total, and line items. Members edit the header and add, change, remove, and reorder items, including decimal quantities and discounts.
- [ ] The difference between the items' sum and the total is always visible, and the draft can be discarded.
- [ ] Unconfirmed drafts are listed so a member can return to them later.

**References:** [#55](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/55), [database schema: attachments and receipts](../../../database-schema.md#proposed-attachments-and-receipts)
