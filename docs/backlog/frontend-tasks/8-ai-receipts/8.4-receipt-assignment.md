# 8.4 Assign receipt items and confirm the expense

**Epic:** [8 AI and receipts](8.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [8.3](8.3-receipt-capture-drafts.md), [8.2](8.2-category-suggestions.md), [#56](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/56)

**Goal:** Members assign each item to the people who had it and turn the receipt into an expense with exact splits.

**Scope:** The assignment screen described in [#56](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/56), using its assignment, preview, and confirm endpoints.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Each item shows name and quantity × price = total, with a row of member avatars. Tapping an avatar toggles an equal share, items with a quantity above 1 get a per-person unit stepper, and a counter such as "2/3 assigned" shows progress.
- [ ] Members can select several items and assign them in one step, or split everything equally.
- [ ] A footer shows each person's total from the [#56](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/56) preview, the unassigned amount, and whether the items match the receipt total. Confirming is unavailable until everything is assigned and matches.
- [ ] Payer, name, date, and category default as [#56](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/56) describes and can be changed. Items stay editable on this screen.
- [ ] Confirming opens the new expense and, when the shopping module is on, offers 8.5.

**References:** [#56](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/56)
