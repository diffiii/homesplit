# 9.2 Suggest categories in the expense form

**Epic:** [9 AI and receipts](9.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [9.1](9.1-ai-opt-in.md), [5.1.4](../5-expenses/5.1-ledger/5.1.4-expense-form-simple.md), [#54](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/54)

**Goal:** The category fills itself in when the AI is confident, without getting in the way.

**Scope:** Calling the [#54](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/54) suggestion endpoint from the expense form.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A suggestion is requested after the name or description stops changing, at most once per distinct input.
- [ ] The category is filled in only when it's empty, the user hasn't picked one, and the confidence is above the [#54](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/54) threshold. It's marked as suggested until the user confirms or changes it.
- [ ] Failures and rate limits are silent and never block saving.

**References:** [#54](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/54)
