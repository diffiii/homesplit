# 2.1 Build sign-up, sign-in, and sign-out

**Epic:** [2 Auth and account](2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [1.5](../1-foundation/1.5-api-layer.md), [1.7](../1-foundation/1.7-app-shell-ui-kit.md)

**Goal:** People create an account and sign in with email and password, and land where they were going.

**Scope:** Sign-up, sign-in, and sign-out with Better Auth's email-and-password endpoints, and the `redirectTo` handling every protected page relies on.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Sign-up asks for name, email, and password, and sign-in for email and password. Better Auth errors (existing email, wrong password, weak password) are shown on the form in plain English.
- [ ] A protected page sends a signed-out user to sign-in with a `redirectTo`, which survives switching to sign-up and is only followed to paths on the same origin.
- [ ] Signing in when already signed in goes straight to the target, and signing out clears cached queries and returns to sign-in.
- [ ] With no households, a signed-in user lands on the 3.1 first-run screen; otherwise on the last household they opened.

**References:** [Better Auth email and password](https://www.better-auth.com/docs/authentication/email-password), [auth config](../../../../packages/backend/src/auth.ts)
