# 2.2 Build the email verification flow

**Epic:** [2 Auth and account](2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [2.1](2.1-sign-up-sign-in.md), [1.4](../1-foundation/1.4-backend-origin-links.md), [#59](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/59)

**Goal:** New users verify their email without getting lost, and the app reflects whether they have.

**Scope:** A "check your inbox" screen, resending the email, the `/verify-email` landing page, and a reminder while unverified.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] After sign-up, the user sees which address the email went to and can resend it, with a cooldown.
- [ ] `/verify-email` shows success, an expired link (with a resend button), or an already used link, and then continues to the original `redirectTo`.
- [ ] Whatever [#59](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/59) decides about signing in before verification is reflected: either sign-in explains that verification is required, or unverified users get a dismissible banner.
- [ ] Verification works when the link is opened on another device or in another browser.

**References:** [#59](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/59), [Better Auth email verification](https://www.better-auth.com/docs/concepts/email#email-verification)
