# 4.3 Invite friends to a household

**Epic:** [4 Friends](4.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [3.4](../3-households/3.4-invitations.md), [4.2](4.2-friends-page.md)

**Goal:** Inviting a friend to a household takes a tap instead of typing their email.

**Scope:** A friend picker in the 3.4 invite dialog.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The invite dialog lists friends who aren't current members and have no pending invitation, searchable by name or email, next to the email field.
- [ ] Several friends can be invited at once with one role. Each invitation's success or failure is shown separately.

**References:** [3.4](../3-households/3.4-invitations.md)
