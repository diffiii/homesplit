# 10.2 Publish the privacy policy and terms

**Epic:** [10 Public pages](10.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [10.1](10.1-landing-page.md), [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53)

**Goal:** Users can read what data HomeSplit keeps, why, and with whom it's shared.

**Scope:** Privacy policy and terms pages. Writing the text itself is outside this task.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The privacy policy covers the data stored, the AI provider and what's sent to it (from [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53)), the email provider, file storage, and the account-deletion rules in the [decision record](../../../decisions.md#deleting-accounts).
- [ ] Both pages are linked from the landing page, sign-up, and account settings.

**References:** [decision record: deleting accounts](../../../decisions.md#deleting-accounts), [#53](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/53)
