# 7.1 Build the shopping list and editing

**Epic:** [7 Shopping](7.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [3.2](../3-households/3.2-household-shell.md), [#50](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/50)

**Goal:** Adding an item takes a few keystrokes, and the list stays readable on a phone.

**Scope:** The shopping list with quick add, editing, and deleting.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A quick-add field stays focused after each item, so several items can be added in a row. Quantity and unit are optional.
- [ ] The unit picker offers `pcs`, `kg`, `g`, `l`, `ml`, and `pack`, plus a custom value, as proposed in [#50](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/50).
- [ ] Items show name, quantity, unit, and who added them, can be edited, and can be deleted.
- [ ] The list refetches on focus and polls while visible.

**References:** [#50](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/50), [shopping contracts](../../../../packages/contracts/src/features/shopping/index.ts)
