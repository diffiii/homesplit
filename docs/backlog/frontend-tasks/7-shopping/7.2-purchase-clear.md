# 7.2 Purchase, unpurchase, and clear items

**Epic:** [7 Shopping](7.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [7.1](7.1-shopping-list-editing.md), [#51](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/51)

**Goal:** In the shop, ticking items off is one tap per item and never loses anything.

**Scope:** Purchase and unpurchase with optimistic updates, a bought section, and clearing bought items.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Tapping an item buys it at once, moves it to a bought section, and offers undo. Failed requests roll back with a message.
- [ ] Bought items show who bought them and when, can be unbought, and can be hidden or shown.
- [ ] Clearing bought items asks for confirmation and reports `deletedCount`.

**References:** [#51](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/51), [shopping contracts](../../../../packages/contracts/src/features/shopping/index.ts)
