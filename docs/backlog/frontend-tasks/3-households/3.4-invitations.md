# 3.4 Send and answer invitations

**Epic:** [3 Households](3.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [3.3](3.3-members-roles.md), [1.4](../1-foundation/1.4-backend-origin-links.md), [#28](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/28)

**Goal:** Admins invite people by email, and invitees join from the email link, even when they don't have an account yet.

**Scope:** An invite dialog, the pending-invitation list, and the `/invitations/:invitationId` page, using Better Auth's `invite-member`, `list-invitations`, `cancel-invitation`, `get-invitation`, `accept-invitation`, and `reject-invitation`.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Admins and owners invite an email address with a role they're allowed to grant. Pending invitations are listed with their expiry and can be cancelled or resent under the [#28](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/28) rules.
- [ ] `/invitations/:invitationId` sends a signed-out visitor to sign-in or sign-up with a `redirectTo` back to the page, including through email verification.
- [ ] Signed in, the page shows the household name and the inviter, and accepts (opening the household) or rejects.
- [ ] A signed-in user whose email doesn't match is told which address the invitation is for and can switch accounts. Expired, cancelled, and already answered invitations have their own messages.

**References:** [#28](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/28), [decision record: Better Auth endpoints](../../../decisions.md#better-auth-endpoints)
