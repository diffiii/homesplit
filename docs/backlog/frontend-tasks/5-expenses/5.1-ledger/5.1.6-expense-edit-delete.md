# 5.1.6 Edit and delete expenses

**Epic:** [5.1 Ledger](5.1.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [5.1.5](5.1.5-expense-form-advanced.md), [#38](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/38)

**Goal:** Authors and admins fix mistakes without breaking the expense's history.

**Scope:** Editing in the 5.1.4 and 5.1.5 form and deleting from the detail page.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Only the author or an admin or owner sees edit and delete.
- [ ] The form opens with the stored values. Only changed descriptive fields are sent, `null` clears them, and `financial` is sent as a whole only when a money field changed.
- [ ] Departed payers and participants already on the expense stay selectable, as the [historical-edit rules](../../../../decisions.md#historical-expense-edits) allow, and newly added people must be current members.
- [ ] Overlapping edits follow the policy [#38](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/38) chooses, and a conflict tells the user to reload.
- [ ] Deleting asks for confirmation, then refetches the list and the balances.

**References:** [#38](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/38), [decision record: historical expense edits](../../../../decisions.md#historical-expense-edits)
