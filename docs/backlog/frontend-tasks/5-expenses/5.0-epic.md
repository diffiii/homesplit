# Epic 5: Expenses

**Backlog:** [Frontend backlog](../../frontend-backlog.md) · **Depends on:** [3 Households](../3-households/3.0-epic.md)

**Goal:** Members record shared expenses quickly, see who owes whom per currency, and settle up.

**Sub-epics:**

- [5.1 Ledger](5.1-ledger/5.1.0-epic.md): categories, list and filters, the expense form, editing and deleting
- [5.2 Settlements](5.2-settlements/5.2.0-epic.md): balances, settling up, and payment history
- [5.3 Attachments](5.3-attachments/5.3.0-epic.md): uploads, expense attachments, and avatars

**Epic done when:**

- [ ] A Playwright test adds an equal expense in PLN and an advanced one in EUR, sees the balances, records the suggested payments, confirms one as the payee, and ends with every balance at zero.
- [ ] The split preview in the form matches the stored splits for every method, including remainders.
- [ ] With the expenses module disabled, expenses, balances, and payments stay readable and no write action is offered.
