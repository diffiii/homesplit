# 5.2.3 Show payment history and confirmations

**Epic:** [5.2 Settlements](5.2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [5.2.2](5.2.2-settle-up.md), [#40](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/40)

**Goal:** Members see every recorded payment, and payees confirm or reject the ones waiting for them.

**Scope:** A paginated payment history using `GET /settlements`, with confirm and delete actions for pending payments.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] History loads pages as the user scrolls and shows payer, payee, amount, note, who recorded it, and whether it's pending or confirmed.
- [ ] The payee, or an admin or owner who isn't the payer, can confirm a pending payment. The payer can withdraw it, and the payee or an admin or owner can reject it. A confirmed payment offers no delete, and 409 `SETTLEMENT_CONFIRMED` is explained.
- [ ] Pending payments waiting for the user are counted in navigation and listed first. If [#40](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/40)'s contract has no way to list them without paging through the history, a filter is proposed there.

**References:** [#40](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/40), [decision record: recording payments](../../../../decisions.md#recording-payments)
