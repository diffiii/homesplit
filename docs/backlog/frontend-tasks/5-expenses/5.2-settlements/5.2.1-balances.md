# 5.2.1 Show balances

**Epic:** [5.2 Settlements](5.2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [3.2](../../3-households/3.2-household-shell.md), [#41](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/41)

**Goal:** Everyone sees at a glance what they owe or are owed, per currency.

**Scope:** A balances page using `GET /balances`.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Balances are grouped by currency and never converted or added across currencies. Each group shows every member's balance, with the user's own highlighted and phrased as "you owe" or "you're owed".
- [ ] Departed members with a nonzero balance are listed with a badge.
- [ ] An all-zero household shows a settled-up state.
