# 5.2.2 Build settling up

**Epic:** [5.2 Settlements](5.2.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [5.2.1](5.2.1-balances.md), [#40](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/40), [#42](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/42)

**Goal:** Recording a payment is one tap from a suggestion, and the form only offers payments the user may record.

**Scope:** Suggestions from `GET /settlement-suggestions`, a payment form for `POST /settlements`, and an admin write-off helper.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Suggestions are listed per currency, with the user's own first. Recording one opens the payment form filled in.
- [ ] The payment form lets current members record payments where they're the payer or payee, and admins and owners between any two household parties. It explains whether the payment will be pending (recorded by the payer) or confirmed at once.
- [ ] Admins and owners can write off a departed member's debt either way the [decision record](../../../../decisions.md#writing-off-debts) describes: forgive it, or split it equally among current members using the shared engine's rounding. This records ordinary payments with the note `Write-off`.
- [ ] After recording, balances, suggestions, and history are refetched.
