# 6.3 Complete and reopen chores

**Epic:** [6 Chores](6.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [6.2](6.2-chore-list-editing.md), [#48](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/48)

**Goal:** Ticking off a chore is instant and records who did it.

**Scope:** Complete and reopen actions with optimistic updates.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Completing and reopening update the list at once and roll back with a message if the request fails.
- [ ] Completed chores show who completed them and when, from the server's response.
- [ ] Completing offers a short undo that reopens the chore.
