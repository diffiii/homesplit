# 6.2 Build the chore list and editing

**Epic:** [6 Chores](6.0-epic.md) · **Depends on:** [3.2](../3-households/3.2-household-shell.md), [6.1](6.1-chore-assignee-filter.md), [#47](https://github.com/diffiii/homesplit/issues/47)

**Goal:** Members see what needs doing and by when, and keep the list up to date.

**Scope:** The chore list with "all" and "mine" views, and create, edit, and delete.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The list shows open chores in the contracted order, can include completed ones, and loads pages as the user scrolls. "Mine" uses the 6.1 filter.
- [ ] Each chore shows its assignee, its deadline in local time (relative when close), and overdue chores stand out.
- [ ] The form sets name, description, assignee (current members only), and an optional deadline entered as a local date and time and sent as UTC. Clearing a field sends `null`.
- [ ] Chores assigned to departed members show the badge and can be reassigned.
- [ ] The list refetches on focus and polls while visible.
